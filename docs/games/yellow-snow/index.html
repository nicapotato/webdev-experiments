❄️ Don't Eat the Yellow Snow! ❄️

Winter survival game! Help our brave character collect white snowflakes while avoiding the... yellow ones!

  • White snowflakes: Safe to eat - collect them for points!
  • Yellow snowflakes: Dangerous! Avoid at all costs!
  • Move left and right to catch the good snow and dodge the bad
  • How high can you score before making a mistake?

🎮 Controls

Arrow Keys / A,D: Move left/right

Space: Restart game • M: Mute/unmute • F: Toggle FPS • ESC: Quit
